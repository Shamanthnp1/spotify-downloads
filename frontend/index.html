How it works

Three steps. No account. No waiting.

  1. 01

    Copy a Spotify URL

    Open Spotify, right-click any track, playlist, or album, and copy its link.

  2. 02

    Paste & choose format

    Paste the URL above, pick MP3, FLAC, M4A, or OPUS — and set your bitrate.

  3. 03

    Download your file

    Click Download. Your file is ready within seconds. No account or payment needed.

Frequently asked questions

What formats are supported?
MP3, FLAC (lossless), M4A, and OPUS. Bitrate options up to 320 kbps for lossy formats.
Can I download full playlists?
Yes. Paste any Spotify playlist or album URL and all tracks will be bundled and downloaded.
Is this free?
Completely free. No account, no payment, no hidden limits.
How long are files stored?
Files are deleted automatically after you download them or within 30 minutes — whichever comes first.
Is this legal?
This tool is intended for personal use only with content you have the right to download. Respect artist rights and local copyright law.
